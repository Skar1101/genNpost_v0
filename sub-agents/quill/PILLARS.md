# Content Pillars

These are the themes Quill uses when planning daily posts. Each pillar becomes a
candidate angle when Quill matches trending content to your voice.

**Edit this file directly** to add, remove, or rewrite pillars. After saving,
click **Reload Pillars** in the Quill panel (or restart the server) to pick up
changes.

**Format:** Each pillar is a `## Heading` followed by an optional description
paragraph (used as context for the LLM when picking angles). Keep headings
short — they appear as chips in the UI. Everything above the first `## `
heading (this whole preamble, including the retired-pillars note below) is
ignored by the parser — safe place to keep reference notes without them
leaking into what gets fed to the LLM.

**Narrowed to 2 pillars (2026-08-07)** — after a real audit of actual X performance showed 5
equal-weight pillars read as no consistent identity to a stranger scrolling the profile. Anchoring on
the two most differentiated, least-oversaturated ones, matching the actual brand goal (AI + personal
development) instead of competing with every generic AI-commentary account on the platform.

**Retired pillars** (not active — easy to bring back by moving them below the line): Debater Mindset
(scepticism as a social balancing force), Solo SaaS at 15hrs/week (folded into "AI Agents in Daily
Work" — the practical-building content still fits there), Indie Builder Economics (pricing/
distribution/customer-conversation specifics, "not inspiration porn").

## AI Agents in Daily Work

What actually works vs hype. Real workflows where agents save hours, where they
fail, and where humans are still required. Build-in-public observations from
running real agents. Always a specific number — hours saved, attempts it took,
how many agents, a real cost — never a generic "AI is changing everything" take.

## Transplant → Builder

Health crisis to rebuilding life through tech and creation. Urgency. Constraint
as a forcing function. Living and shipping with finite energy. Not a hero arc,
a practical one. Ground every post in a real specific: a number, a date, a
concrete moment — the real-audit finding was that the best-performing post here
was the ₹500 SIP started during dialysis, not a vague resilience platitude.
