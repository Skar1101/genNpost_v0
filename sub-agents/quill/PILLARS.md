# Content Pillars

These are the themes Quill uses when planning daily posts. Each pillar becomes a
candidate angle when Quill matches trending content to your voice.

**Edit this file directly** to add, remove, or rewrite pillars. After saving,
click **Reload Pillars** in the Quill panel (or restart the server) to pick up
changes.

**Format:** Each pillar is a `## Heading` followed by an optional description
paragraph (used as context for the LLM when picking angles). Keep headings
short — they appear as chips in the UI.

---

## Debater Mindset

Debaters are always a balancing part of society. In one way they kill many
potential ideas before those ideas get a fair hearing — but in another way they
protect society from catastrophe by stress-testing what's about to spread.
There's a tension worth exploring: when does scepticism serve, and when does it
suffocate?

## Solo SaaS at 15hrs/week

Building software businesses on the side as one person, using AI as the team.
What changes when you can't throw people at a problem. What you ship vs what
you cut. The economics of being deliberate.

## Transplant → Builder

Health crisis to rebuilding life through tech and creation. Urgency. Constraint
as a forcing function. Living and shipping with finite energy. Not a hero arc
— a practical one.

## AI Agents in Daily Work

What actually works vs hype. Real workflows where agents save hours, where they
fail, and where humans are still required. Build-in-public observations from
running real agents.

## Indie Builder Economics

Pricing, distribution, customer conversations, the boring stuff that compounds.
Specific numbers, not "I made $X this month" inspiration porn.
