## ENGAGEMENT FARMING TEMPLATE (refined from examples)

Hook: Big outcome or tool reveal
Body: 3 bullets of what it does
Offer: What you're giving away free  
CTA: "Comment [KEYWORD] + follow → I'll DM it (must be following)"

Proven CTA keywords from examples: VIDEO, CODE, CLAUDE, SWIPE, SDR, M, AUTO, BUILD

---

## KEY PATTERNS ACROSS ALL WINNERS

1. Hook in line 1 — no warm-up, no context
2. Specific numbers always beat vague claims
3. Lowercase casual voice outperforms formal
4. Bullets and line breaks = higher completion
5. "Must be following" creates follow without asking directly
6. No disclaimers, no hedging, no "I think"
7. End with a punch or a CTA — never fade out
8. Curiosity gap in hook → deliver in body
9. Pain point first, then solution
10. Short sentences after long ones = rhythm that keeps readers scrolling
