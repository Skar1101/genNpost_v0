## 2. Virality Breakdown (What Actually Works)

| Element          | Formula Used                          | Psychological/Algo Trigger                  | Result |
|------------------|---------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|--------|
| **Hook**        | Emoji + bold claim + curiosity gap   | Stops scroll instantly                      | High CTR |
| **Timeliness**  | "Just dropped", "48 hours", "This week" | FOMO + newsjacking                          | Early momentum |
| **Pain → Solution** | One relatable frustration         | Immediate relatability                      | Bookmarks |
| **How-To**      | 3-bullet maximum steps               | Zero friction to try                        | "Trying now!" replies |
| **Proof**       | Stars count + "100% free/local"      | Trust + anti-hype signal                    | Reposts |
| **Visual**      | One clean screenshot                 | Proof + aesthetic                           | Higher completion |
| **Length**      | 8–15 lines (single post)             | Mobile-perfect                              | Algo boost |

**Engagement Flywheel**: Early likes → algo push → community replies → quotes → sustained views.
