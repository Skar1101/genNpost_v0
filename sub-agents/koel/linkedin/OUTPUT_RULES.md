# LinkedIn — output rules

- **Hashtags: 3-5, at the very end, on their own line.** Specific ones (`#PlatformEngineering`,
  `#SaaSPricing`), never generic ones (`#motivation`, `#success`, `#leadership`). This is the one
  platform in this system where hashtags are correct — X and Substack ban them outright.
- **No URLs in the post body.** If a link matters, end with a line saying it's in the comments.
- **Paragraphs of 1-3 sentences, blank line between each.** Never a solid block.
- **First two lines carry the claim.** Everything before the "see more" fold must make someone want
  the rest. No setup, no scene-setting, no "I've been thinking about…".
- **~150-300 words.** Under 150 reads as an X post that wandered in. Over 300 loses the scroll.
- **Close on something responder-worthy** — a real question, or a claim someone could disagree with
  productively. Never "Agree?", "Thoughts?", or "Who else?".
- **No thread numbering.** No `Tweet 1/`. There are no threads here.
- **No DM-keyword CTAs.** That mechanic is X-only.
- **Write about the idea, not the memoir.** Even for career and building-in-public topics, lead
  with the observation rather than Souvik's personal narrative. His story can inform the post; it
  should rarely be the subject of it.
