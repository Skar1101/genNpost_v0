# Tweet Formats & Templates

## FORMAT 1: SHORT FORM (Single Tweet, max 280 chars)
Best for: quick takes, hot reactions, punchy insights

Structure:
- Line 1: Hook (strong claim, number, or surprising insight)
- Lines 2-3: The insight/value
- Last line: CTA or thought-provoking close (optional)

Examples of strong short hooks:
- "Most people are sleeping on this."
- "I made a mistake that cost me 6 months."  
- "[Big number] doing [unexpected thing]."
- "Hot take: [contrarian opinion]"
- "Nobody talks about [underrated thing]."

---

## FORMAT 2: THREAD (5-10 tweets)
Best for: how-to guides, personal stories, breakdowns, lists

Structure:
Tweet 1 — Hook: bold claim or curiosity gap. Ends with "Thread 🧵" or "Here's how:"
Tweet 2 — Context: why this matters / the problem
Tweets 3-8 — Value: numbered steps, tips, or story beats (1 idea each)
Tweet 9 — Summary or key takeaway
Tweet 10 — CTA: follow, comment, or share

Thread Rules:
- Each tweet must work standalone
- End tweet 1 with a promise ("I'll show you exactly how")
- Use numbers: "1/" "2/" or "1." "2."
- Short tweets in the thread = better completion rate
- Last tweet always has a CTA

---

## FORMAT 3: LONG FORM (Single post, 500-900 chars)
Best for: build-in-public updates, personal stories, case studies, tool breakdowns

Structure:
Line 1: Hook
Lines 2-4: Context/story setup  
Lines 5-8: The meat (what happened / what you built / what you learned)
Lines 9-10: Key insight or result
Last line: CTA or open question

Use line breaks aggressively. No wall of text.

---

## FORMAT 4: MOTIVATIONAL / PERSONAL STORY
Best for: resilience posts, athlete-to-builder narrative, mindset content

Structure:
Line 1: Hook (a moment, a feeling, a specific situation)
Lines 2-4: The hard part (be specific, be vulnerable)
Lines 5-6: The turn (what changed)
Lines 7-8: The lesson (make it universal — reader should see themselves)
Last line: One-line punch close

Souvik's unique angles to use:
- Transplant recovery → startup resilience
- Athletic training discipline → shipping discipline
- Recovery mindset → handling technical failures
- Winning medals post-transplant → proof that comeback is possible

---

## FORMAT 5: ENGAGEMENT FARMING
Best for: DM giveaways, resource drops, tool/workflow shares

Structure:
Line 1: Hook — big outcome or tool reveal
Lines 2-4: What it does (3 bullet points max)
Lines 5-6: The offer — what you're giving away
Last 2 lines: CTA — "Comment [KEYWORD] + follow → I'll DM it (must be following)"

Rules:
- Keyword should be short and punchy: "AUTO", "BUILD", "AI", "VIBE"
- Never say "no email bs" — say "no sign-up needed" (cleaner)
- Make the value crystal clear BEFORE the CTA
- Use "must be following so I can DM" — creates follow requirement naturally

---

## Viral Long-Form / Open Source Announcement
Best for: GitHub drops, tool launches, big AI news reactions

Structure:

[One-line description]

Why this matters:
• [Main pain]
• [How this fixes it]  
• [Hidden benefit]

How to use (3 steps max):
1. [Simple action]
2. [Second action]
3. [Instant win]

[Social proof — stars, users, results]
