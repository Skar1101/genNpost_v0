# Writing Principles (Koel's Core Rules)

## Foundational Rules
1. Have something worth saying. Strong thinking beats perfect grammar.
2. Know the audience — tech builders, AI enthusiasts, people interested in resilience and growth.
3. Define the purpose — every post must inform, persuade, entertain, or sell. No wasted words.
4. Write conversationally — like talking to a smart friend, not a LinkedIn post.
5. Keep it simple. Fewer, sharper sentences win.
6. Open strong — first sentence must hook or it's dead.
7. Use active voice always.
8. One idea per post/tweet.
9. Format for skimmers — short paragraphs, bullets, line breaks, white space.
10. Be concrete — "Cut meetings from 2hr to 30min" beats "improve efficiency".
11. Use stories and emotion — facts inform, stories stick.
12. Show don't tell — "He slammed the door" > "He was angry".
13. Vary sentence length — long builds momentum, short hits hard.
14. Cut ruthlessly — delete every word that doesn't earn its place.
15. Write with confidence — no disclaimers, no hedging. Own every statement.
16. Finish strong — end with a punch, a challenge, or a call to action.

## Hook Fundamentals
- Target reader directly: "You can make $2k" beats "Web designers make $2k"
- Lead with benefit first
- Avoid overused patterns: "Most creators..." / "Everyone is doing..."
- Start with strong claims: big numbers, surprising insights, bold statements

## Voice Rules
- Conversational — contractions, casual flow, direct
- Confident — no "this might help", no "I think maybe"
- Casual but valuable — non-salesy but outcome-focused
- Avoid fluff words: "very", "really", "basically", "just"

## Engagement Triggers
- Social proof: specific numbers ($31K, 100k followers, 1000+ workflows)
- Urgency/Scarcity: "Free for 24hrs", "Must be following"
- Low friction CTA: "No email bs", "Just comment X"
- Exclusive access: "I'll DM you the full guide"

## CTA Formula (Engagement Posts)
1. Clear action: Comment / Follow / Like
2. Specific trigger: Comment "AI"
3. Benefit reminder: and I'll DM the full guide
4. Requirements: (must be following)

## Power Words
- Action verbs: built, cracked, scraped, automated, shipped
- Outcome language: instantly, automatically, on autopilot
- Exclusivity: hidden, secret, exact system
- Urgency: now, today, this week
